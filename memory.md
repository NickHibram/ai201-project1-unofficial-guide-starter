## Question
What is a violin made out of

## Answer
According to **HANDBOOK OF VIOLIN PLAYING**, a violin is constructed from several types of wood: the upper table (top plate) is made of pine; the back, ribs, neck, and bridge are made of maple; the sound post and bass bar are usually pine as well. The fingerboard and pegs are most commonly fashioned from ebony, though box wood and rosewood are also frequently used for the pegs.

## Question
What are they made out of

## Answer
According to HANDBOOK OF VIOLIN PLAYING, violin strings are made from the intestines of sheep. The lowest string is wrapped with fine wire—silver wire being considered the best, though copper wire is also commonly used because it is much cheaper.
